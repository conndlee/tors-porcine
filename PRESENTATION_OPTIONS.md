# Manuscript presentation

- **Case IDs:** P01–P07 in chronological order. See [case labels](CASE_LABELS.md).
- **Reading order:** background, surgical methods, evaluation, procedure results, visual field obstruction, suturing, then discussion and limitations.
- **Figures:** overview, procedure timelines, visual field obstruction, and combined suturing analysis. See [figure plan](FIGURE_PLAN.md) for sources and widths.
- **Preview:** two-column `journalpreview` layout with a left-aligned title and centered purple uppercase headings. Citation and figure links are light blue; DOI text uses the reference font.
- **Submission:** an unstructured abstract, a concise presentation of the innovation, and a discussion of its significance. The body uses two main sections without subsections, with the conclusion in the closing Discussion paragraph.

Analysis tables and supplementary material are excluded from the paper. Formatting limits are in the [README](README.md#repository-submission-guidelines).
