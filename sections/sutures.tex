The recordings contained 62 annotated stitches: 23 with 3 mm instruments and 39 with 8 mm instruments. Suture placement time was measured from pickup of the suture needle to tightening of the final throw. The interval from final knot tightening to suture cutting by the assistant (knot-to-cut interval) was recorded separately (\cref{fig:suture-phases}).

Mean suture placement time, calculated by averaging the animal-specific means, was 7.1 minutes with 3 mm instruments and 4.1 minutes with 8 mm instruments.

At the individual-suture level, pooled median needle-passage times were 121.8 seconds with 3 mm instruments and 71.3 seconds with 8 mm instruments. Median knot-tying times were 147.1 versus 123.4 seconds. The phase distributions and knot-to-cut intervals are shown in \hyperref[fig:suture-analysis]{\Cref*{fig:suture-analysis}a}; stitches were clustered within pigs.

Both groups had a median of three throws per knot; pooled median knot-tying time per throw was 52.6 versus 43.5 seconds (\hyperref[fig:suture-analysis]{\Cref*{fig:suture-analysis}b--c}). Knot-tying time per throw was total knot-tying time, including suture pull-through, divided by the number of throws.
