Across all procedures, total successful sutures were 23 with 3 mm instruments and 39 with 8 mm instruments. Suture time was measured from pickup of the suture needle to tightening of the final throw. The interval from the final knot tightening to the suture cutting by the assistant (knot-to-cut interval) was recorded separately (\cref{fig:suture-phases}).
Mean suture time was 7.1 minutes with 3 mm instruments and 4.1 minutes with 8 mm instruments.
For the individual sutures, the median needle-passage time was 121.8 seconds with the 3 mm instruments and 71.3 seconds with the 8 mm instruments. Median knot-tying times were 147.1 versus 123.4 seconds. The timing distributions and knot tightening-to-cut intervals are shown in \hyperref[fig:suture-analysis]{\Cref*{fig:suture-analysis}a}.

Both groups had a median of three throws per knot with median knot tying time per throw observed as 52.6 versus 43.5 seconds for the 3 and 8 mm tools respectively (\hyperref[fig:suture-analysis]{\Cref*{fig:suture-analysis}b--c}). Knot tying time per throw was the total time from bite to final tightening motion, divided by the number of throws.
