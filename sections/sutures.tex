\WIPsubsection{Suture Timing}
The seven main recordings contained 62 annotated stitches: 23 with 3 mm instruments and 39 with 8 mm instruments. Stitch duration ran from first needle--tissue contact to the last knot throw being tightened. Time from tightening to the assistant's cut was recorded separately (Fig.~\ref{fig:suture-phases}).

The mean of the per-pig mean stitch durations was 7.1 minutes with 3 mm instruments and 4.1 minutes with 8 mm instruments. Median procedure duration, from dissection onset to the last retained cut or knot endpoint, was 115.91 versus 75.46 minutes (Table~\ref{tab:sutures}). These descriptive results did not demonstrate faster suturing with the smaller instruments. Figure~\ref{fig:procedure} shows the full annotated procedures, including dissection and repair phases.

At the individual-suture level, pooled median needle-passage times were 121.8 seconds with 3 mm instruments and 71.3 seconds with 8 mm instruments. Median knot-tying times were 147.1 versus 123.4 seconds. The phase distributions and assistant intervals are shown in Figure~\ref{fig:suture-phases} and Table~\ref{tab:suture-phases}; stitches were clustered within pigs.

Needle passage and knot tying were also summarised by annotated repair region in the draft supplement (Table~\ref{tab:suture-regions}). Both groups had a median of three throws per knot; the pooled median knot interval per counted throw was 52.6 versus 43.5 seconds (Table~\ref{tab:knot-throws}). Per-pig statistics from the stitch overview are retained in Table~\ref{tab:suture-by-pig}. Motion data were available for 58 stitches and are reported separately (Table~\ref{tab:phase-motion}).
