\WIPsubsection{Suture Timing}
The seven main recordings contained 61 annotated stitches: 23 with 3 mm instruments and 38 with 8 mm instruments. Stitch duration ran from first needle--tissue contact to the last knot throw being tightened. Time from tightening to the assistant's cut was recorded separately (Fig.~\ref{fig:sutures}).

The mean of the per-pig mean stitch durations was 7.1 minutes with 3 mm instruments and 4.2 minutes with 8 mm instruments. Median procedure duration, from dissection onset to the final cut, was 115.91 versus 75.46 minutes (Table~\ref{tab:sutures}). These descriptive results did not demonstrate faster suturing with the smaller instruments.
