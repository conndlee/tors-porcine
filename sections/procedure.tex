\WIPsubsection{Annotated Procedure}
The main comparison included seven annotated procedures. Figure~\ref{fig:procedure} shows dissection, repair phases, stitches, and elapsed time for each recording. Median procedure duration was 115.91 minutes with 3 mm instruments and 75.46 minutes with 8 mm instruments (Table~\ref{tab:sutures}). The endpoint was the last retained cut or knot-tightening event; the final P01 assistant interval was excluded.
