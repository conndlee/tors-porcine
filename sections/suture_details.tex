\WIPsubsection{Instrument Motion}
Motion analysis included 19 stitches from two 3 mm pigs and 39 stitches from four 8 mm pigs; P01 had no rosbag. Signals were averaged into 0.05-second bins.

\begin{itemize}
\item Jaw closure was defined as closing below 0.1 rad after opening above 0.3 rad; this did not establish a successful grasp.
\item Both instruments were classified as still when each shaft end moved below 2 mm/s and wrist rotation below 0.1 rad/s.
\item Path length, rotations, and jaw closures were summed across instruments. Workspace was the sum of separate shaft-end convex-hull volumes in instrument-relative frames; it did not represent anatomical or tool-tip workspace. Spread was averaged across instruments.
\item First-60-s workspace excluded phases shorter than 60 seconds; brackets give retained sample sizes.
\end{itemize}
