\WIPsubsection{Instrument Occlusion}
Instrument masks covered shafts, wrists, and jaws in the left endoscopic view. Occlusion was sampled at one frame per second.
The median of the recording medians was 10.39\% with 3 mm instruments and 23.93\% with 8 mm instruments (Table~\ref{tab:occlusion}). With occlusion below 1\% excluded, the corresponding values were 10.71\% and 24.42\%. The pooled-frame density plot used this latter filter and had medians of 11.0\% and 25.1\% (Fig.~\ref{fig:occlusion}).
