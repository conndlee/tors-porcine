\WIPsubsection{Instrument Occlusion}
Occlusion was defined as the image fraction occupied by instrument shafts, wrists, and jaws, excluding the retractor, teeth, needle, and suture. The left endoscopic view was cropped below the title bar and analysed at one frame per second. A DeepLabV3--ResNet-50 model was fitted using 120 labelled frames; 30 frames were held out within the same six recordings. Median test intersection-over-union was 0.921 for 3 mm instruments and 0.973 for 8 mm instruments. Median absolute image-area error was 0.33 and 0.20 percentage points, respectively. One tool-free test frame had a false-positive instrument area of 1.66\% and an intersection-over-union of zero.

Whole-recording median occlusion ranged from 10.25\% to 11.31\% for 3 mm instruments and from 22.86\% to 26.50\% for 8 mm instruments (Table~\ref{tab:occlusion}). These descriptive values include all sampled video intervals; they are not suturing-specific estimates. Camera distance varied, and the test split did not assess generalization to unseen recordings. \textbf{[Task-matched occlusion results and camera-view assessment.]}
